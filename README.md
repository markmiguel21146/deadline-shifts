# Deadline Shifts
A 2D psychological horror game developed in Unity, combining typing-based gameplay, point-and-click exploration, and narrative-driven environmental interactions.

The game follows a night-shift transcription technician working for a fictional government department responsible for processing classified and anomalous records. What begins as a routine data-entry assignment gradually unfolds into a disturbing investigation involving unexplained incidents, conflicting documents, and information that appears to acknowledge the player's presence.

Inspired by The King in Yellow by Robert W. Chambers, the game explores themes of forbidden knowledge, perception, and the unsettling consequences of uncovering information that was never meant to be understood.

# Key Features
- **Typing-Based Gameplay** – Transcribe classified documents and meet assigned work quotas.
- **Point-and-Click Exploration** – Interact with objects and investigate fixed 2D environments.
- **Narrative-Driven Progression** – Discover the story through documents, computer logs, and environmental events.
- **Psychological Horror** – Experience increasingly disturbing anomalies as the story progresses.
- **Environmental Interactions** – Investigate unexpected events and interact with objects to advance through each shift.
- **Progressive Storytelling** – Uncover connections between classified records, unexplained phenomena, and the department's activities.
